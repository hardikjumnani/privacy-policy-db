# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 6, 2026**

Pass the Sprouts is an Android application published by **Hardik Jumnani**, an individual publisher based in India.

This Privacy Policy explains how Pass the Sprouts handles information when you use the app.

By using Pass the Sprouts, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Handle

Pass the Sprouts is designed to work primarily on your device. It does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, home address, date of birth, or other identity information.

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle the following information.

### Meal and nutrition information

The app may store information you enter or generate while tracking meals, including:

* Meal dates and times
* Meal type
* Food names
* Food quantities and units
* Estimated calories
* Protein, carbohydrates, fat, and fibre values
* User-entered nutrition overrides
* Meal notes
* Nutrition goals
* Meal status and related local records

This information is primarily stored locally on your device.

### Workout information

If you use workout tracking, the app may store:

* Workout labels that you enter
* Calories you enter for a workout
* Workout date and time

Workout information is stored locally and is not sent to Gemini by Pass the Sprouts.

### Meal photographs

If you use the camera feature, Pass the Sprouts may capture and store photographs of your meals.

Photos are stored in the app's private storage. The app does not request access to your general photo library or public device storage.

Because you control what you photograph, a photograph may unintentionally contain other people, documents, screens, or other personal information. You should avoid photographing sensitive or identifying information.

### Gemini API key

If you use AI features, you provide your own Google Gemini API key.

The key is stored locally using Android's secure storage mechanisms. Pass the Sprouts does not send the key to the publisher, does not include it in exports, and does not store it in the app's meal database.

The key is used to authenticate requests to Google's Gemini API when you use an AI feature.

### Purchase and entitlement information

Pass the Sprouts offers a one-time ₹59 purchase that unlocks weekly and monthly calorie reports and removes advertisements.

Payments are processed through Google Play Billing. Pass the Sprouts does not receive or store your payment card, UPI details, billing address, Google account email, or other payment credentials.

The app stores only the local entitlement state necessary to determine whether the purchased feature is unlocked. Google Play remains responsible for the purchase transaction and restoration of the purchase.

### Advertising and technical information

Pass the Sprouts uses Google AdMob for advertisements.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google's advertising systems, Google may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address or network information, approximate location inferred from network information, and information relating to advertisements and interactions with advertisements.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.

Users who purchase the ₹59 ad-removal feature do not receive advertisements.

## 2. How We Use Information

Pass the Sprouts uses information for purposes including:

* Recording and displaying your meals and nutrition information
* Recording workouts
* Generating calorie and nutrition estimates
* Producing weekly and monthly calorie reports
* Storing your preferences and goals
* Providing AI-powered meal analysis and calorie estimation
* Enabling user-requested export, import, and sharing features
* Providing the purchased report and ad-removal features
* Displaying advertisements where permitted
* Maintaining the security and functionality of the application

Pass the Sprouts does not use your information to provide medical diagnosis, medical treatment, or medical advice.

## 3. AI and Google Gemini

Pass the Sprouts allows you to use your own Gemini API key to access Google's Gemini API.

When you use an AI feature, information necessary for that feature may be transmitted directly from the app to Google.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

* Meal photographs
* Food names
* Food quantities and units
* Other information required for the AI request

The app does not intentionally send workout labels, workout records, meal history, calorie goals, or user identifiers to Gemini as part of its current AI functionality.

Meal notes are stored locally by the app. The app does not currently populate the optional AI hint field with meal notes.

### Important information about unpaid Gemini API services

The current Google Gemini API Additional Terms state that when Unpaid Services are used, Google may use submitted content and generated responses to provide, improve, and develop Google products, services, and machine-learning technologies. Google also states that human reviewers may read, annotate, and process API inputs and outputs for quality and product improvement purposes.

Accordingly, **do not submit personal, sensitive, confidential, medical, financial, authentication, or other private information to Gemini through Pass the Sprouts.**

Google's current Gemini API terms should be reviewed before using the AI features because Google's terms and data practices may change.

Pass the Sprouts does not control Google's independent processing of information after it is transmitted to Google.

Google may process or store relevant Gemini data outside India.

Pass the Sprouts uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform is used to obtain and manage consent.

You can manage or withdraw applicable advertising consent through the privacy controls provided by Google's consent system.

Google may independently process advertising and device-related information in accordance with its applicable policies.

## 6. Google Play Billing

The ₹59 one-time purchase is processed through Google Play Billing.

Pass the Sprouts receives the purchase/entitlement result necessary to unlock the purchased functionality.

The app does not store:

* Payment card details
* UPI details
* Billing address
* Google account email
* Google account identifier
* Order ID
* Purchase token

Google Play handles purchase restoration after reinstall where applicable.

The purchase entitlement is intentionally preserved when you use Pass the Sprouts' Delete All Data function.

## 7. Export and Import

Pass the Sprouts provides user-controlled local export and import functionality.

An export may contain:

* Meal records
* Food and nutrition information
* Meal photographs
* Image thumbnails
* Workout entries
* Nutrition goals
* Settings
* Meal notes

The Gemini API key and purchase entitlement are not included in exports.

Full-resolution original photographs are not included in the current export format. If you have enabled the full-resolution-photo setting, the export may contain a reference to an original file without containing that original file itself.

Import provides two modes:

### Merge

Merge preserves the existing data on your device and adds imported meals whose identifiers do not already exist.

Existing conflicting meals are not overwritten.

### Replace All

Replace All explicitly deletes the existing meal database and app-controlled image data before restoring the imported archive.

Replace All does not delete your Gemini API key or your Google Play purchase entitlement.

Export and import do not automatically upload your data to cloud storage.

## 8. Sharing

Pass the Sprouts allows you to share an individual meal using the Android system share sheet.

Sharing occurs only after you explicitly choose the Share action.

Depending on your settings, the shared content may include:

* A watermarked meal photograph
* Meal and nutrition information
* Food names and other information contained in the meal record

Pass the Sprouts does not choose the recipient or destination application for you and does not receive information about which application or person you ultimately share the content with.

After you share information with another application or person, that copy is outside Pass the Sprouts' control and is governed by the recipient's or destination application's own practices.

## 9. Local Storage

Pass the Sprouts stores app data primarily in private application storage on your Android device.

Meal and workout databases are stored locally. Meal photographs are stored in private app storage.

The app does not use a Pass the Sprouts cloud database or backend server for storing your meal history.

Pass the Sprouts does not provide automatic cloud synchronization of your meals, photographs, workouts, goals, or exports.

## 10. Backup

The production version of Pass the Sprouts is intended to disable Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for app data.

This is intended to prevent Pass the Sprouts' local database and photographs from being automatically transferred through Android backup mechanisms.

## 11. Deletion

Pass the Sprouts provides a **Delete All Data** function.

When used, it is intended to permanently delete app-controlled user data, including:

* Meals
* Food entries
* Meal photographs
* Thumbnails
* Workout entries
* Nutrition goals
* Notes
* Settings
* The locally stored Gemini API key

The Google Play purchase entitlement is intentionally retained so that a user does not lose a purchased feature when deleting app data.

Temporary app-generated files are intended to be deleted as part of the data-deletion process. Android may independently manage temporary/cache storage.

If you uninstall Pass the Sprouts, Android normally removes the app's private application data. Pass the Sprouts does not retain a server-side copy of your local meal database or photographs.

Information that you voluntarily share with another application or person may remain outside Pass the Sprouts' control after sharing.

Information already transmitted to Google through Gemini, AdMob, or Google Play may also be retained or processed by Google according to Google's applicable policies and terms. Deleting local app data does not retroactively delete information that another service has independently received.

## 12. Security

Pass the Sprouts uses reasonable technical measures appropriate to the app's architecture, including Android private application storage, secure storage for the Gemini API key, and encrypted HTTPS connections for supported network communications.

The local meal database is not individually encrypted at the application level.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Android device and any Gemini API key you provide.
